Privacy policy

MUSUBI (“we”, “us”) respects the personal information you share with us and handles it in line with applicable data protection laws. This policy explains what we collect, why, and how we protect it.

1. Information we collect

Through our contact form we collect:

  • Required: your name and email address
  • Optional: company name, phone number, the topic and details of your enquiry

2. How we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry and contact you
  • To prepare proposals and quotes, and to enter into and perform contracts
  • To improve our services and tell you about new ones

3. How long we keep it

We keep enquiry data for one year after your enquiry is resolved and then delete it. If we enter into a contract, we keep it for the contract term and any period required by law.

4. Sharing with third parties

We do not share your personal information with third parties without your consent, except where required by law.

5. Service providers

We may use trusted service providers (for example, for hosting and email) to process data on our behalf, only as needed and under appropriate supervision.

6. Security

We use access controls, encrypted connections and other reasonable measures to protect your information against loss, misuse or unauthorised access.

7. Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete your personal information, or ask us to stop using it, at any time. We will respond promptly after confirming your identity.

8. Cookies and analytics

We may use analytics tools to improve this website. They use cookies to collect anonymous traffic data that does not identify you. You can turn cookies off in your browser settings.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Any changes take effect when they are posted on this page.

10. Contact

For questions about your personal information, please use our contact form.
