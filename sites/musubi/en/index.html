06 — FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Can I just have a consultation?

Of course. The first consultation is free and comes with no obligation. Even if you’re not sure where to start, feel free to get in touch.

How much does it cost?

It depends on the scope and length of the work. We’ll propose a few plans that fit your situation, so let us know your rough budget first.

Do you work with small businesses and sole traders?

Yes. We work with businesses of any size and focus a limited budget on the work with the biggest impact.

Can you work with us remotely?

Yes. Meetings are mostly online, so we can work with you wherever you are.

Can we hire you just for ads, or just for a website?

Absolutely. You can hire us for only the part you need — and if we see something more urgent in the bigger picture, we’ll tell you honestly.
